## NI-JAP's Main Objective
NI-JAP is a cultural and community platform that aims to build a strong bridge between Niger (and West Africa) and Japan through anime, manga, and cultural exchange.

* Specific Objectives:

Promote Japanese culture in West Africa using anime and manga as an accessible and engaging tool for young people.
Create an active otaku community in Niger and the region (discussions, announcements, quizzes, duels, events).
Facilitate cultural exchange between Niger and Japan (learning the Japanese language, discovering traditions, organizing events).
Support talented Nigeriens passionate about Japan (MEXT scholarship applications, study opportunities, partnerships).
Promote Nigerien culture by introducing it to Japanese and international audiences.

# Short Version
NI-JAP: The cultural bridge between Niger and Japan.

We use our passion for anime and manga to bring our two cultures closer together, build a strong community, and open up opportunities for young West Africans.
